Exchequer Risk Management · A partnership proposal for accountancy principals

A referral partnership — properly structured, properly rewarded.

Exchequer works with a small, deliberately-limited group of accountancy principals across the UK. Our partners refer commercial clients to us when insurance is on the table — a new incorporation, a change of premises, a renewal that isn't landing right. In return, they receive documented, ongoing remuneration on every policy those introductions produce. It is a long-relationship arrangement, not a transactional one.

Signed off personally by Trevor Roberts, Managing Director.

01What the arrangement gives you

Recurring remuneration

Compensation on every policy — and every renewal.

Not a one-off finder's fee. A documented commission on the policies your introductions place with us, continuing for as long as those clients remain on cover. A steadily compounding income line as your book of introductions grows.

Your clients stay yours

No cross-sell. No approach to the wider book.

We handle the specific brief you introduce — nothing else. Your clients aren't contacted about adjacent services, and we make no attempt to reach anyone else in your practice.

Trevor's personal oversight

You deal with the MD, directly.

Every partnership sits under Trevor's personal signature. You aren't handed to an account team — you have a working relationship with the MD, and every introduction is placed with his sign-off.

Worth noting

The relationship can flow both ways. When one of our clients comes to us needing a corporate accountant, we know where to send them. The arrangement, however, stands on its own regardless — the commercial value to your firm sits in the introductions you make to us.

The arrangement · The person · The next step

Built on long relationships — not one-off referrals.

Nothing goes live until both sides have agreed scope and commercials in writing under an FCA Introducer Appointed Representative framework. The first conversation with Trevor is exploratory — no commitment, no paperwork, no obligation to proceed.

02Four steps from first conversation to live

i.

Introductory conversation

Trevor. 15 to 20 minutes. Informal — often over coffee if you're in reach of Coventry. He listens more than he talks. No commitment on either side.

ii.

Terms drafted

If both sides see the fit, Trevor drafts the arrangement — commission structure, scope, exclusivity terms, renewal treatment, quarterly review cadence.

iii.

Reviewed, agreed, signed

Your compliance function reviews, ours reviews, terms are agreed in writing under the FCA IAR framework. Nothing verbal, nothing informal.

iv.

Live, with quarterly review

First introductions typically flow within 30 days. Trevor reviews the relationship personally each quarter — commercials, volume, fit.
